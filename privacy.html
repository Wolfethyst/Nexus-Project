Privacy Policy

How Nexus handles your data and messages across Twitch, YouTube, and Nexus.

Overview
Last updated: [11/30/2025]

Nexus is an interactive experience operated by Wolfethyst (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use Nexus, including integrations with Twitch, YouTube, and other services.

TL;DR: we use your data to run chat, points, redeems, Talathyst (AI), and moderation tools. We do not sell your data, and we only share it with services we use to power Nexus (like Twitch, YouTube, Cloudflare, OpenAI, and ElevenLabs) or when required by law.

1. Who operates this service?

The Service is operated by Wolfethyst, an individual creator and streamer.

Contact for privacy questions:

  • Email: nexus@wolfethyst.tv
  • Website: https://nexus.wolfethyst.tv

2. What information do we collect?

2.1 Account & profile information

When you log in or use Nexus, we may collect:

  • An internal Nexus user ID (UUID)
  • Your display name / username
  • Your avatar or profile image URL
  • Your Nexus display name and name color (if customized)
  • Linked platform identifiers (Twitch, YouTube, Google)

2.2 OAuth and platform data (Twitch / YouTube / Google)

When you connect via Twitch or YouTube, we use OAuth to obtain limited access to your account for features like chat integration and channel information. We do not receive or store your platform password.

2.3 Chat and moderation data

For Nexus, Twitch, and YouTube chats handled by Nexus, we store:

  • Message text and timestamps
  • Your user ID and display name
  • The platform the message came from

For moderation, we may also keep warnings, timeouts, bans, related moderator information, and a short history of messages used to justify moderation decisions.

2.4 Points, redeems, and activity tracking

To power leaderboards and redeems, we track message counts, watch time increments, points balances, and point events (message, watch, redeem, bonuses, moderator adjustments).

2.5 Talathyst (AI assistant)

When you talk to Talathyst, we send your message and a minimal conversation history to the OpenAI API to generate a reply. We store this history for a limited time to maintain context. Please do not send sensitive personal information in Talathyst messages.

2.6 TTS (Text-to-Speech)

For TTS redeems, we send your text to a TTS provider such as ElevenLabs to generate audio. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

2.7 Automatically collected information

Our infrastructure, including Cloudflare, may log IP addresses, request metadata, error details, and basic analytics for security and performance. We do not use this information for third-party advertising.

3. How do we use your information?

  • To operate chat, points, redeems, overlays, and dashboards
  • To provide Talathyst AI responses and TTS audio
  • To moderate the community and prevent abuse
  • To debug, secure, and improve Nexus
  • To comply with legal and platform requirements

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your data. We may share information with:

  • Infrastructure providers (Cloudflare Workers, KV, D1, R2, Stream)
  • AI and TTS providers (OpenAI, ElevenLabs) when you use those features
  • Twitch / YouTube as part of chat and streaming integrations
  • Authorities when legally required

5. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary to operate Nexus, maintain points and leaderboards, moderate chat, and comply with legal or platform requirements. Chat and AI history may be trimmed or anonymized over time.

6. Cookies

We use cookies (like wolfesession) to keep you logged in and secure the Service. They are not used for third-party ad tracking.

7. Children’s privacy

Nexus is intended for users aged 13+ or the minimum age in your country to use Twitch / YouTube. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us at nexus@wolfethyst.tv. We may need to verify your identity.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your data, including HTTPS and Cloudflare security features. No system is 100% secure, so avoid sharing highly sensitive information through chat or Talathyst.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date. Your continued use of Nexus after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
